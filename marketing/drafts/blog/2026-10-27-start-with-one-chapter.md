Scenewright turns a novel into a comic with AI-assisted adaptation and illustration. Before you commit to a whole book, start with a chapter or a short story and judge the result.

Choose a passage that gives you something useful to evaluate. A conversation can show whether the characters' expressions carry the scene. A change of setting can show whether the comic makes the transition clear. You do not need to begin with the most spectacular scene in the manuscript.

Keep a short review list. Can a reader follow the action without the original prose beside it? Is the text readable? Does the page preserve the part of the scene that matters to you? These are questions to ask of the result, not guarantees about what a generative system will produce.

Finished comics export as PDF or CBZ. Choose the format that suits how you plan to read or share the work, then review that exported file as well as the pages in the studio.

The first chapter is a chance to decide what you want the comic to be before you take on the rest of the book.
