# Scenewright marketing copy kit

**Status: Draft, September 26, 2026.** Nothing here has been sent, posted or paid for. Pairs with the v3 video set ([EDIT-v3.md](../video/EDIT-v3.md)) and the [growth strategy](../../docs/proposals/showcase-and-growth-2026-09.md).

## House rules

- Show real output only. Say "AI-assisted" plainly; the author directs, the studio produces.
- No prices, credits, free generations or model names in any public copy or media.
- Never name an artist as a style ("in the style of…"). Describe medium, era and mood instead.
- Keep "Your novel. Any style." as the one signature line; write everything else as normal sentences rather than stacks of two-word fragments.
- Credit sources every time: *The Masque of the Red Death*, Edgar Allan Poe (public domain); *Kaitlin Out of Space*, Krishan Coupland (CC0); *Site-30's Janitor*, JanitorCakeworth, SCP Wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).
- Ads never use Janitor pages 4 onward (gore) or the last panel of the gouache edition's page 1 (profanity).

## Carousel: "One Poe scene, ten styles"

1. Cover: the woodblock page. Text: "We asked for ten versions of the same Poe scene. Swipe."
2–10. One proof per slide, each labeled with the direction exactly as typed, e.g. "Two-color linocut print in black and blood red on cream paper."
11. The chosen look as a finished page. Text: "We picked the woodblock for the full book. Which would you have chosen?"
12. CTA slide: "Your novel. Any style. scenewright.ink"

Alt text pattern: "Comic page from The Masque of the Red Death: Prince Prospero seals the iron gates of his abbey, drawn as a [style]."

## "Comment a style" format

- **Post:** the woodblock page with the caption "Comment a style and we'll draw this Poe scene in it. Weirder is better."
- **Reply video (7–10 s):** the commenter's words typed into the direction box, then the proof slamming onto the board. On-screen: "@name asked for [style]."
- **Rules:** make only proofs we'd be happy to show; skip any request that names a living artist, a brand or a real person, and reply with an alternative medium instead.

## Serialized classic (October)

Post one finished page of the Masque edition each day with a single line of Poe underneath it and the page number. The last page goes up on October 31. Pin a comment linking to the free public reader.

## Author Spotlight outreach

> Subject: Could we turn your opening chapter into a comic?
>
> Hi [name], I loved [book/serial]. I build Scenewright, a comic studio for fiction writers, and we're making a small Spotlight series: the opening chapter of ten indie books, adapted into finished comic pages with the author approving the look and cast. You would see every page before anything is published, you'd keep full control over whether and where it appears, and you could share it with your readers however you like. If you're open to it, reply and I'll send the details and a written permission form. If not, no worries at all.
