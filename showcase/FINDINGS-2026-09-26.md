# Product findings from the showcase refresh

Started September 26, 2026, while building the v3 showcase and marketing kit. These are issues to address after the showcase work. Anything that blocked the showcase was fixed in place; those items are marked **fixed in place**.

| # | Area | Finding | Impact | Suggested follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Look · style suggestions | Generated suggestion names cite artists, including living ones: "Mignola Block Shadow", "Wrightson Gothic Pen and Wash", "Gorey Crosshatch Tableau" (Masque); "Jansson-Inspired Domestic Gouache", "Kollwitz Charcoal Witness" (Kaitlin); Janitor editions were built from "Edward Hopper" templates. | Brand and trust risk: "in the style of <living artist>" is the most-cited criticism of AI art. The names also reach public books, screenshots and marketing captures. | Name directions by medium, era and mood rather than people. Keep artist lineage out of user-facing names and out of stored templates. |
| 2 | Pages · editable lettering | Kaitlin Out of Space (the landing page's showcase book) cannot open lettering: `409 Stored comic page is incompatible with the current pipeline. Rerun Plan to rebuild it.` Rerunning Plan would invalidate all 59 drawn pages. | Older editable books are stuck with first-draft placement, even for pure placement fixes that don't touch Draw. | Offer a migration that rebuilds the comic-page record without changing art, or let placement-only edits proceed on the existing lettering state. |
| 3 | Pages · lettering seeding | Kaitlin page 11: the speech balloon "You should take those off." has no tail. | The reader can't tell who is speaking; this page is on the landing page (`kaitlin-page-11.webp`). | Replace the landing asset; seed a tail for every onscreen speaker. |
| 4 | Pages · lettering seeding | Site-30's Janitor (gouache, editable): intercom and other offscreen voices were seeded as plain balloons; the v11 "radio" voice exists but wasn't applied. Onscreen tails pointed at shoulders or hands instead of mouths. One balloon sat on the speaker's head (page 1, panel 5; page 3, panel 2). | First-draft pages look amateurish until hand-fixed. **Fixed in place** on pages 1–3 (lettering revision 4, marked reviewed after visual check). | Derive the radio/electric voice from speech tags such as intercom, phone or radio at seeding. Aim default tails at the head or mouth, not the element's nearest edge. |
| 5 | Pages · placement evidence | Janitor pages 1–3 have no face ("heads") evidence (`page_read` null), so placement suggestions can't avoid faces. | Placement relies on guesswork on exactly the pages that need it. | Run the free page read on draw completion, or make face detection a prerequisite for suggested placement. |
| 6 | Look · style directions | `POST /styles/direction` returns `409 Wait for all project jobs to finish` while a six-suggestion batch runs (about 15 minutes). | The author can't type their own idea during the slowest step of the Look room. | Queue author directions behind the batch instead of rejecting them, or say in the UI that directions will open when the batch finishes. |
| 7 | Marketing assets | The landing hero loop (`hero-flipbook.mp4`) and the 65-second promo lead with Dungeon Crawler Carl; the promo also uses The Egg. Both are copyrighted; `landing-development-samples.json` says DCC is "Not yet approved for public campaign". | Legal exposure and ad-platform rejection once paid traffic starts. | Replace with the v3 cleared-source assets (this work). |
| 8 | Marketing assets | `janitor-gouache-page-01.webp` on the landing page predates the lettering fixes in #4 and contains profanity in its last balloon. | Stale, weaker page and an avoidable content flag. | Regenerate from the reviewed composite; crop or choose a different page for paid placements. |
| 9 | Look · style proofs | Masque proof 27 ("Cross-stitch sampler on linen") failed at the provider after the batch; 25 of 29 proofs matched their direction, and three came back as generic painting (picture-book watercolor, neon noir, ink-and-wash). | Authors can't tell a proof that ignored their direction from one that followed it. | Offer "try again" on a failed or off-target proof, and consider a cheap direction-adherence check before showing it. |
| 10 | Pipeline · queue | After cast approval, Adapt (text only) waited behind the automatic project cover image. | Minutes of avoidable wait between two author checkpoints. | Run cover generation at lower priority, or after Draw starts. |
| 11 | Pages · lettering API | Tails on offscreen speakers are rejected (`422 Offscreen copy and captions cannot point to a visible speaker`), which is correct, but the editor doesn't suggest the radio voice for intercom or phone lines. | Offscreen voices look like ordinary balloons with no tail. | Suggest the radio or whisper voice when a line is offscreen. |
| 12 | Pages · corrections | A benign costume-colour correction on Masque page 10 ("change the dancing woman's white gown to deep emerald green") was silently declined by the image provider (`image_tool_failed`, classified `provider_blocked`). The user message is clear that work is unchanged, but gives no way forward. | Authors hit a dead end on ordinary art-direction notes and can't tell what to rephrase. | Suggest a rephrase or a narrower region, and say that page context (here, a revealing costume nearby) can trigger the refusal even when the note is harmless. |
| 13 | Pages · corrections | When a correction fails before any image exists (here a Codex transport disconnect), its tracked issue stays open, and resubmitting the same note creates a second issue rather than answering the first. The failure message also says "try this panel again", although Draw now makes complete pages. | Duplicate issues pile up after failures, and the copy describes a workflow that no longer exists. | Offer "Try again" that reuses the open issue, and change "panel" to "page" in the service-unavailable message. |
| 14 | Pages · lettering (in-progress work) | Uncommitted changes in the shared checkout (changed 11:01, adding `translation_sources` to `LetteringElement`) change the lettering content hash, so every previously saved editable page now fails validation: `Stored page lettering is incompatible with the current pipeline`. Affected: all Masque pages except 18, Janitor gouache 1–3, Kaitlin 1–3. Manual issues from failed corrections also can't be dismissed (`only an automated Draw finding can be dismissed`). | Lettering can't be edited, and Finish, export and publication fail for these showcase books until the pages are re-stamped. The v3 videos are unaffected; they use composites rendered before the change. **Fixed in place** for Masque (23 pages) and Janitor gouache (9 pages): a one-off re-stamp that adds only `translation_sources: {}` and refuses any other content difference; both books now pass Finish. The failed-correction issues on Masque pages 3 and 10 were accepted through the API. | When that work lands, re-stamp the showcase projects' lettering once (as with the v11 upgrade), then re-check composites. Allow dismissing a manual issue whose correction failed. |
| 15 | Plan · schema change | Commit `0de352e` (Sept 25) added `camera` and `acting` to planned panels, so Kaitlin Out of Space and Site-30's Janitor (risograph) fail to load their page plans (`Stored comic page is incompatible… Rerun Plan`). Every render stores its plan's hash, so rebuilding the plan marks all drawn pages stale. | Neither book can be finished, exported or published without re-running Plan and Draw (56 and 7 pages). Their already-rendered art still works in the videos and the landing page. | Decide whether to redraw these two editions, or accept that older showcase books stay unpublishable after breaking plan changes. |
| 16 | Pages · lettering | Janitor gouache page 1 (intercom caption) and page 2 ("Thank you, Janitor Worth.") failed Finish's strict fit check although the editor preview drew them without complaint. | The editor lets authors save layouts that Finish later rejects. | Run the strict fit check in the editor, or flag overflow before save. Fixed in place by widening both boxes. |
| 17 | Pipeline · rerun | Rerunning Plan deletes `plan/`, `draw/` and `finish/` outright (`_clear_outputs_for_rerun` → `shutil.rmtree`); only Adapt reruns archive their work. Kaitlin lost all 59 drawn pages and its finished book; the risograph Janitor lost 7. The Plan estimate showed nothing about this, and a rerun that then fails leaves the book with no art at all. | Irreversible loss of paid artwork from one request, with no warning or undo. Surviving copies are in `showcase/archive/`. | Archive drawn pages and finished books before clearing (as Adapt does), keep them viewable until the new Draw succeeds, and require explicit confirmation that names the number of pages that will be discarded. |
| 18 | Plan · model limits | Kaitlin's Plan rerun on the Claude subscription failed at 2/15 with a raw provider message ("You've hit your session limit · resets 8pm"); the risograph rerun on `claude-opus-5-5` through the gateway failed with Anthropic's "compiled grammar is too large… reduce the number of strict tools". | Book-length Plan can't run on Opus 5.5 through the API route, and a subscription limit surfaces as an unexplained failure. | Split or loosen Plan's strict tool schema for Anthropic routes (or exclude that route for Plan), and translate subscription-limit errors into a "try again after <time>" message. |
| 19 | Pages · lettering seeding | Elm and Keep: five seeded sound effects ("KA-BOOM!", "hssssss…", "AAAAAIIIEEE!", "WEEEOOOO WEEEOOOO", "poom… poom…" on pages 10, 15, 16, 18, 19) got boxes 5% of the page tall, below what the style's 44 px minimum SFX size needs. Their saved layout records `overflow: true`, yet the editor showed no warning, and Finish then rejected the reviewed book. A sixth ("HEE-HEE-HAHAHA!", page 10) only surfaced after the first was fixed, because Finish reports one failing element per page. **Fixed in place**: boxes raised to 7.5% of the page; page 15's moved off the caption. | An author finishes a full lettering pass and still can't publish, with no hint in the editor. | Size seeded SFX boxes from the style's minimum font size and warp, and surface `overflow` in the editor (see #16). |
| 20 | Showcase · packaging | `scripts/prepare_showcase.py` failed on every editable-lettering book: the showcase manifest had no `locale`/`translations` fields and the packager kept the author-only `unpublished_locales`. **Fixed in place** (manifest gains optional languages; packager strips author-only fields; test added). The running API needs a restart to load the new manifest format; until then the local homepage shelf returns 500. | Blocked Masque, Kaitlin and Janitor gouache from the homepage shelf. | Done; restart the server. |
