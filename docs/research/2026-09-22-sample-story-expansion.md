# Modern sample story expansion: source audit

Research date: 2026-09-22. This is a source and selection note, not an import manifest. At the start of the audit, the bundled shelf had six works: five by Krishan Coupland and Angie’s *An Education*. The table below identifies **29 additional candidates**. The implemented catalog now includes 15 of them, for **21 curated stories** total: the first 14 recommended entries plus *A seed drifts by*. “Length” is a rough reading-size estimate from the live source page unless an exact TXT word count or publisher reading time is stated; the catalog calculates final counts from extracted text. The links in each row are original publisher pages with the complete article or story text. No source artwork is bundled.

## License and extraction rules

- Coupland marks the listed works [CC0 on each story page](https://krishancoupland.co.uk/project-tag/copyright-free-stories/). His TXT downloads for *Men of the Waste* and *Dumb Creatures* returned HTTP 200 on this research date; *The House of My Grandfather* was readable as plain text. The author page is the license evidence; the TXT is the content source.
- [Wanderverse](https://wanderverse.org/) declares the collaborative project CC0. The individual *Elm and Keep* publisher page identifies the commissioned author as **@strnglft**, and *A seed drifts by* identifies **Sonya Mann**. Preserve those bylines rather than treating the site owner as author of every contribution.
- The SCP Wiki [Licensing Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide) explicitly permits reposting full articles and making adaptations, including commercial adaptations, provided the specific article and author and the wiki are attributed and the derivative work is released under **CC BY-SA 3.0**. This is materially different from CC0: a generated comic adapted from an SCP story must have a durable attribution and share-alike notice when exported or shared. A source link on the picker alone is insufficient for that output. The [image policy](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/image-use-policy) concerns media separately; extract article text only and omit page photographs, drawings, audio, styles, and third-party media. In particular the Licensing Guide warns against commercial use of the former SCP-173 sculpture likeness.
- SCP pages mix narrative text with menus, author notes, citation boxes, footnotes, and sometimes interactive sections. Define the story boundary per page. Retain the article’s actual story, logs, and in-world documents in order; omit wiki furniture and license-box text from the **story body**, while keeping their provenance in catalog metadata. Some article-style entries will need a framing note so users understand they are adapting a fictional case file.

## Holdbacks, not counted above

- [Days Necrotic](https://krishancoupland.co.uk/portfolio/days-necrotic/) and [The Little People](https://krishancoupland.co.uk/portfolio/the-little-people/) have author CC0/waiver notices, but their official TXT download links returned **404**. They are good future additions if a full authorized text source is recovered; do not fabricate missing text.
- [Blue](https://krishancoupland.co.uk/portfolio/blue/) is CC0 and its TXT is accessible, but the story includes explicit sexual activity involving a young character; exclude it from the default sample shelf.
- [Soul Juice](https://www.sonyasupposedly.com/soul-juice/) is a CC0 contribution by **GABA girl**, but it explicitly continues a Wanderverse serial. The same is true of the [Sam Wilson gnome/Nanomancer chapters](https://www.sonyasupposedly.com/gnome-nanomancer-apprentice/) (CC0); avoid presenting chapters as complete stories.
- [Date Night](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/date-night) is CC BY-SA text but revolves around SCP-173. The [SCP licensing guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide) specifically warns that the old sculpture likeness is not available for commercial adaptation. Its comic value is not worth an accidental visual-reference failure.
- [The Steward](https://repository.lib.fsu.edu/islandora/object/fsu%3A982175) by Camille Thomas is listed CC BY 4.0 at Florida State University, but the repository did not expose the complete text to this research session (403). Verify access to the actual work before adding it.
