# Narrative review and advisory length

**Status: Accepted, September 12, 2026.** Supersedes the minimum-page-floor policy in [ADR 0007](0007-source-accounted-adaptation.md).

A 22-beat adaptation allocated all its words by page 22, then repeated its final beat across twelve silent pages to meet a 34-page minimum. Global lettering preservation and panel-variety checks accepted it. Page targets now express preferred scale, not a floor or ceiling: Plan chooses the complete, purposeful length, preserves the requested target as metadata, and explains its allocation.

Adapt and Plan require a separate, evidence-cited narrative review before publishing canonical output. Source-window reviews retain global story order and neighboring beats; Plan reviews global allocation before detail on larger books, then actual page content. Computed silence and beat-concentration signals require explicit assessment but do not automatically prohibit intentional visual storytelling.

**Review policy amended September 13, 2026:** subjective pacing, padding,
emphasis, panel variety, and page-turn preferences remain advisory. Negative
signal assessments do not become blockers. Substantive narrative findings may
block only with a concrete reader consequence, reader-impact explanation, and
verifiable quotations tied to supplied artifact IDs. Unsubstantiated claims are
retained as optional notes. Structural and explicit-requirement validation stays
strict, and an invalid review never counts as approval.

This trades mandatory polishing for fewer subjective regeneration loops. Each
stage attempt allows one targeted narrative repair; Plan no longer escalates
unresolved detail findings to a new whole-book allocation. Remaining blockers
retain the draft and evidence for explicit review. These are judgments admitted
under an evidence contract, not proven literary facts or a quality guarantee.

Persist versioned review evidence against exact input content. New drawing requires current successful Adapt and Plan reviews; old artifacts without evidence require rerunning their owner, without compatibility exceptions. Read-only inspection and saved-image OCR remain available. This adds metered text work and can produce false positives or negatives. It buys explicit scrutiny of narrative meaning before image spend, without claiming to guarantee artistic quality or replacing human review.

Review contract `narrative_v4` supplies supporting source text for detailed beat
and lettering citations, accepts lettering IDs as evidence, and groups repeated
ledger decisions losslessly. A global signal is assessed once by a window that
contains its entire sequence. Fixed neighbor windows cannot bound reader knowledge:
a review incorrectly requested a date already printed just outside its window.
Every window now includes the remaining exact adapted lettering as compact reader
context, without duplicating its detailed beats. This trades larger text requests
and broader invalidation after copy edits for complete evidence of established
reader knowledge, without another generation loop. Canonical content hashes still gate publication;
window checkpoints use their actual evidence payload, permitting unchanged
windows to be reused. Malformed Adapt review evidence stops the attempt rather
than authorizing a story rewrite. Persistent per-input structured-request limits
bound explicit resumes as well as in-attempt repair; these are work limits, not
provider-dollar guarantees. See the current stage contracts for admission and
estimate details.
