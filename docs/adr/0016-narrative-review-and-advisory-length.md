# Narrative review and advisory length

**Status: Accepted, September 12, 2026.** Supersedes the minimum-page-floor policy in [ADR 0007](0007-source-accounted-adaptation.md).

A 22-beat adaptation allocated all its words by page 22, then repeated its final beat across twelve silent pages to meet a 34-page minimum. Global lettering preservation and panel-variety checks accepted it. Page targets now express preferred scale, not a floor or ceiling: Plan chooses the complete, purposeful length, preserves the requested target as metadata, and explains its allocation.

Adapt and Plan require a separate, evidence-cited narrative review before publishing canonical output. Source-window reviews retain global story order and neighboring beats; Plan reviews global allocation before detail on larger books, then actual page content. Computed silence and beat-concentration signals require explicit assessment but do not automatically prohibit intentional visual storytelling. Unresolved review findings block publication and drawing, and enter the owning stage's bounded repair workflow. A probabilistic editorial finding is not a proven literary invariant; the blocking contract is that required review remains unresolved.

Persist versioned review evidence against exact input content. New drawing requires current successful Adapt and Plan reviews; old artifacts without evidence require rerunning their owner, without compatibility exceptions. Read-only inspection and saved-image OCR remain available. This adds metered text work and can produce false positives or negatives. It buys explicit scrutiny of narrative meaning before image spend, without claiming to guarantee artistic quality or replacing human review.
