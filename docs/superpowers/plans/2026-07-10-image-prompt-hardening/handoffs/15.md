## Contract delivered

- Added version 1 `VisualEvaluationRecord`, `LabsVisualExperimentRecord`,
  `PairedScenarioRecord`, `VisualExperimentProposal`, and `PairedVisualReport`
  contracts in `novel_to_comic.labs.visual_evaluation`.
- Added versioned rubric `visual-evaluation-rubric-v1`. Character results are
  keyed and validated by entity id; immutable identity and mutable appearance
  state are distinct. Location identity/state, prop identity, style,
  composition, missing/merged/duplicated/extra subjects, and readable text are
  separate evidence-backed dimensions.
- Evaluation attribution retains scenario, route, compiler/wire versions,
  execution fingerprint, selected/dropped references, evaluator identity,
  reviewer notes, cost, and latency. Successful/failed/missing artifacts retain
  request provenance and failure evidence.
- Labs-only experiment storage lives at
  `_labs/visual_experiments/{experiment_id}/experiment.json` and `report.json`.
  Experiment writes require the typed versioned model; reads validate it.
- Pair validation rejects scenario/route mismatches. Retained-baseline compiler
  selection is explicitly rejected outside Labs; no production old/new toggle
  was added.
- Pure dry-run estimation performs no provider call. The balanced default is 8
  scenarios × 2 repeats: 0 prompt calls, 16 candidate image calls, and 32
  evaluator calls.
- Reports preserve missing/failed pairs in denominators and analyze by route and
  failure class. They calculate paired means, baseline standard deviation,
  material-regression floors, standard error, and 95% descriptive intervals.
  Executable decisions enforce canonical regression, +0.15 alternate-state,
  route-wide systematic-failure, minimum-sample, and complete-class gates.
