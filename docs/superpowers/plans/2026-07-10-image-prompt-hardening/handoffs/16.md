## Remaining risks and next-slice prerequisites

- There is no next slice. Provider retry normalization, binary image-format
  validation (including JPEG bytes mislabeled `.png`), automatic cross-model
  failover, runtime capability discovery, and broader continuity remain the
  explicitly deferred reliability work.
- Reference-slot constraints remain route-specific. Required visible subjects
  stay in text with explicit dropped-reference diagnostics; no provider may
  silently omit or reorder them.

  manifest and invalidating abandoned prepared requests; exact replay is lost
  if referenced content-addressed blobs are removed.
