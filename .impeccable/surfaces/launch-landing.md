# Launch landing

Mode: Persuade. Extend the established Living Ink landing rather than replace its visual world. The first purchase is a short excerpt made into a first comic scene; the author can expand after reviewing the result. Preserve the ink, pulp, red and gold palette, editorial type, physical paper stacking and existing signup path.

Route `/` is implemented by `web/src/pages/HomePage.tsx`, `HomePage.css` and `landingCopy.ts`. The copy is maintained in English, Spanish and French. `.landing-hero` introduces the first-scene offer; `.landing-premise` explains a manageable excerpt. `#continuity` and `.landing-page-samples` put two consecutive, uncropped comic pages before the workflow. Both images and their caption links open the full-size files. The sample grid is two columns on desktop and one below 680px. `.landing-reference-inspection` connects the evidence to author review and `/signup`.

`#process` explains the brief, style/cast, page review and export checkpoints. `#rights`, `.landing-trust` and `#questions` describe creative control, AI assistance, credits without a subscription, variable production cost and limitations. Primary conversion links use the existing `/signup`; returning visitors use `/login`.

Current provisional page assets are `/marketing/dcc1-2-page-003.webp` and `/marketing/dcc1-2-page-004.webp`, from the dcc1.2 development output. They are English pages containing strong language, labeled as provisional development work rather than customer work. Current image imperfections are accepted for this launch iteration; a future model change is separate work.
